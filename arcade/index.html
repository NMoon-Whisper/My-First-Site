Legal library policy

Only titles with auditable redistribution rights belong in the catalog. This initial section ships one original, locally implemented browser game; no third-party arcade ROMs or box art are bundled. Add a title only after its rights holder, license, source, and attribution have been verified and recorded in games/ and LICENSE.md.

Local ROM loading is a user-controlled technical feature, not a statement that any particular file is legal or compatible.
